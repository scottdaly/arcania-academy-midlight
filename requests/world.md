World always checks `hasSprite` and falls back, so a missing key never shows magenta.

### ui
- `game.world.getObjective(game)` returns a one-line current objective (also in `level.data.objective` in the hub).
  Showing it in the pause menu / HUD would help new players. World toasts it on entering the hub.
- Dialogue: node text is kept <= 125 chars. The shop and some menus show up to 8 choices. Choices whose
  `requires` fail are hidden (the shop only lists what you can afford; "What do you stock?" lists all prices).
- World opens the healer's dialogue automatically ~0.7 s after a respawn (`from: 'respawn'`). No other dialogue
  opens by itself (so new game, boss fight and victory are never blocked by a panel).
- Dialogue-less NPC lines (boss taunts, floor intros, ambush alerts) are toasts.

### combat
- Enemies are spawned only via `game.combat.spawnEnemy(game, id, x, y, {elite?})` / `spawnBoss`. World does not
  pass `hpMult` - combat's `difficulty()` already scales by depth / floor.
- Ids used: dungeons `armor_knight armor_lancer armor_sentinel armor_captain golem_stone golem_crystal golem_frost
  golem_ember wisp_ember wisp_frost wisp_storm`; tower waves use the same set. Unknown ids are filtered out.
- `world.spawnPickup` also accepts kind `'gear'` (resolves to an unowned staff/robe of the current tier, or
  arcane fragments) so `{kind:'gear'}` enemy drops work.
- Loot tiers (world/loot.js): 1 `staff_oak robe_scholar`; 2 `staff_ember staff_frostbite staff_geode
  robe_emberweave robe_frostweave robe_stormweave`; 3 `staff_stormcaller staff_prism robe_battlemage`;
  4 `staff_archmage robe_archmage`. Chests prefer gear you do not own; duplicates become arcane fragments.
- Gifts: Headmaster 2x `potion_health`; Brindle +3 fire/+3 ice (on top of your starting fragments) and later
  `robe_battlemage`; Wren `robe_scholar`; Corvin `robe_archmage`. Shop prices are in `SHOP_STOCK` (dialogue.js).
- Spells that hit a dungeon's loose stone (PROJECTILE_HIT from the player, or an AOE_TRIGGERED nearby) open the
  secret room. Requires `projectile.team === 'player'` or `projectile.caster === game.player`.

### world-private events
- `world:buy {itemId, cost}` - fired by the quartermaster's dialogue `emit` actions; world spends + gives.
